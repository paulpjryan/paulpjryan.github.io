    Setup

    Strum and watch the meter. Set the line just above the room noise so only real strums count.

    Pick the same device as your input when you can, such as your interface’s mic input and its headphone jack. Two separate devices run on separate clocks, and the delay between them slowly drifts.

    Latency
    Not calibrated

    Takes about 8 seconds. Turn the click volume up, mute your strings and hold still. The page plays a burst of clicks and times how long each takes to come back through your mic. Using headphones? Hold one earbud or earcup against the mic while it runs. Recalibrate whenever you change your input, output or headphones.

    ms

    Added to the measured delay. Leave at 0 after calibrating.

    2 & 4 mode

    The click sits on the backbeat like a snare. The count-in plays all four beats first so you know where 1 is.

    Cleaner readings

    Muted or choppy strums have sharp attacks and read best. Long ringing chords can hide the next strum’s attack.

    Reading the spread

    Under 8 ms is studio tight. 8–15 ms is solid. Above 25 ms the groove starts to feel unsteady to listeners.
